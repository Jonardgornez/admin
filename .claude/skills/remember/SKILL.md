---
name: remember
description: Recalls what happened in previous sessions. Use at the start of a session, or when the user types /remember.
allowed-tools: Read
---

1. Read `.claude/memory/activity-log.md`
2. Summarize:
   - What was worked on last session
   - What files were touched
   - What was completed vs still in progress
   - Any decisions made and why (check decisions.md too)
3. If the log is empty, say so plainly — don't invent history.
4. End with: "I'm ready to continue. Here's where we left off:"
   followed by the summary.
